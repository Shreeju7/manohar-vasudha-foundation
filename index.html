Curriculum

Three theory papers and three practical papers, as per the course syllabus.

Module 1: Anatomy, Physiology & Pathology
  • Human body introduction
  • Cells, tissues, organs and body regions
  • Musculoskeletal system: bones, joints, muscles
  • Digestive system, metabolism, carbohydrate, protein and nutrition
  • Circulatory system
  • Respiratory system
  • Endocrine system
  • Nervous system
  • Excretory system: kidney and skin
  • Reproductive system
  • Pathology
  • Laboratory pathology and investigation report reading
  • Introduction to bacteriology, virology, fungi and parasitology
  • Case study of patient
  • Approach to patient, history taking and case-sheet writing
